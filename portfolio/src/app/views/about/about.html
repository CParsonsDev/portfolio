Product Development

With Print & Play Print & Play , I made ( literally thousands ) of prototypes for game developers.

Game Development and Prototyping

Many of these games were for individuals and teams new to game development that needed support translating their assets into something real. As a leader in the physical production of games and components, I aided support staff in evaluating product feasibility, production timelines, and design specifications.

Others were for the biggest names in entertainment who knew exactly what they wanted - whether we’d done it or not. Rapid prototyping and process engineering was necessary to competitively meet these customers' needs, which was always an exciting challenge that demanded optimal interdepartmental coordination.

My work was split between product design, machine programming and operation, team coordination, hand making artisanal handicrafts, and playing games.

You can see the variety of standard offerings we’d offered if you go to the Wayback Machine Print & Play , but we really made anything.

Team Development

People > Process > Execution. Repeat.

Leadership and Collaboration

I have a strong background in leading teams and fostering collaboration to achieve project goals. I believe in creating an inclusive and supportive environment where everyone can contribute their best work.

Check in with the people you’ll impact, ensure you understand that the process meets everyone’s needs, then execute that process better than you did last time. Everything done well follows this sequence, and every team craves it.

When I was a supervisor in retail, following this sequence meant that I had my teams’ support through the highs and lows. It meant that the team was able to avoid infection through the COVID pandemic. When I was out, I was able to trust that my team would get the work done on time and on budget safely.

When I led custodial teams across my local school district, it meant that I was adaptable to the circumstances of each school, their ongoing events, and their community’s expectations - and that the support I offered to teams was mutual.
